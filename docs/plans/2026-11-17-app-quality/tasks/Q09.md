## Observed gap

savePreferences publishes units/offline/layers and launches an unawaited save. Rapid combined changes and failures have no explicit per-action lifecycle.

## Implement

Serialize or supersede preference writes so the latest combined intent wins. Show pending/failure/retry adjacent to the changed preference and use explicit rollback or retained-unsaved state.
