## Observed gap

App.tsx uses a fixed-height header and a single 960px breakpoint. Empty Explore exposes inactive filters before a search. Its global message is one dismiss button, making content/status semantics weak. Current 390px Explore had no document overflow.

## Implement

Adapt header/navigation and Explore empty-state hierarchy to narrow screens and large text. Give global notices readable content and a separate dismiss action with appropriate status semantics.

## Acceptance criteria

1. At 320/390/768/1280 widths, title, watch entry and tabs remain reachable with no document overflow.
2. Empty Explore emphasizes starting/opening a search and downloading a map; inactive controls do not dominate.
3. Notice text is announced once and remains readable independently of its dismiss control.
4. Navigation and closing a search preserve independently verified active watch status.
