## Acceptance criteria

1. Failed saves retain previous committed data and current editable drafts. Failed deletions truthfully report unchanged versus partial cleanup, prevent opening missing assets and recover through idempotent retry/reload.
2. Exported app reloads offline only after verified shell readiness; selected cached maps remain usable where actually downloaded.
3. Retained expired evidence keeps its original dates; newly fetched evidence is assessed on its own provenance. Pre-commit interruption preserves the previous complete pack. Interruption during commit resolves from the durable active pointer without rolling back a successful activation. A failed shell update retains the last complete verified shell.
4. Failures are deterministic test-only injections with no live provider dependency; native/offline claims remain separate.
