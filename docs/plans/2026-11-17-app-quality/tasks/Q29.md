## Acceptance criteria

1. Cancellation accepted before commit prevents activation and stale UI success. Unmount during commit cannot interrupt a successful durable write or claim cancellation.
2. Previous active pack survives pre-commit cancellation/failure; cleanup never deletes assets referenced by a committed active pointer, including after an uncertain response.
3. Watch replacement protection is rechecked immediately before activation.
4. Retry starts a coherent operation and cannot activate an obsolete response.

## Focused verification
