## Observed gap

## Implement

Expose read-only shell lifecycle status plus retry and accurate update guidance. Keep Metro unsupported and preserve verified activation behavior.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Shell ready/not-ready/error is separate from map/evidence availability.
2. A waiting update explains close/reopen behavior without forcing reload or skipWaiting.
3. Failed update keeps the previous complete shell usable and data intact.
4. Native/Metro surfaces do not claim browser service-worker readiness.
